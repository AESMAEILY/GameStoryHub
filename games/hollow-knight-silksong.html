Story & lore

Captured and carried far from Hallownest, Hornet wakes in the haunted, silk-choked kingdom of Pharloom. Compelled by a mysterious summons ringing from its highest peak, she cuts her way upward through crumbling cathedrals, bogs, and bell-towns tangled in an ancient, decaying faith. Old gods, needle-cults, and a fractured royal line all have a claim on the kingdom's fate, and on the ringing bell that seems to be calling her by name.

Capture and Arrival

Hornet is ambushed by robed Choir cultists who bind her in silk and carry her across the sea to Pharloom, a ruined religious kingdom she has never seen. She breaks free of her captors and falls into the Moss Grotto at the foot of the kingdom's central peak, stripped of her cloak and needle. Realizing her abductors served a holy order based in a great Citadel far above, Hornet resolves to climb Pharloom's pilgrim road to its summit and confront whoever ordered her capture.

The Pilgrimage Up Pharloom

As Hornet ascends through Pharloom's decaying farmlands, bogs, and factories, she finds a once-great empire crumbling under 'the Haunting,' a spreading madness that reanimates the dead and turns the living violent. Along the way she recovers her tools and gathers allies and rivals alike, including a mysterious bell-ringer, a knowing snail shaman, and the lethal, cult-like Sisters of Battle stationed throughout the land. Rumors keep surfacing of a slumbering 'Mother' whose silk threads are somehow bound to every soul in the kingdom.

The Weavers' Secret

Hornet eventually uncovers Pharloom's founding myth: long ago, a Higher Being called Grand Mother Silk arrived and elevated ordinary spiders into a race called the Weavers, telling them they were her divine daughters. When the Weavers realized this was a lie, they used song to lull Grand Mother Silk into an eternal sleep and sealed her within a hidden sanctum called the Cradle. Generations later, the Citadel of Song was built atop her prison to keep her dormant, its people unaware they remained silently bound to her by the silk running through their own bodies.

Lace and the Waking Mother

Denied her original Weaver children, Grand Mother Silk spun a new one from pure silk: Lace, a fast and dangerous rival who crosses Hornet's path again and again, sometimes hunting her, sometimes helping her. As Hornet nears the Citadel's summit, the Mother's influence grows stronger and the Haunting spreads into open corruption across Pharloom, threatening to permanently bind every remaining bug's soul and shell to her will. Lace, resentful of the mother who made her only to be used, ultimately turns against her and helps free Hornet from captivity.

The Confrontation in the Cradle

At the climax, Hornet reaches the Cradle to face Grand Mother Silk, and the outcome depends on what she has accomplished along the way. In the simplest ending, Hornet defeats the Mother outright and takes her place, becoming a new Weaver Queen fused with silk and ruling the Citadel herself. By completing further quests and crafting a Soul Snare with the Citadel's Caretaker, she can instead trigger the game's true path: playing the Needolin drags Grand Mother Silk toward the Void, and Lace, finally rebelling against her creator, wounds the Mother and frees Hornet — only to be dragged into the abyss herself.

Into the Void

With Grand Mother Silk falling, Pharloom's Haunting-corrupted enemies are consumed by the Void and the kingdom edges toward collapse. Hornet dives into the Void itself to confront 'Lost Lace' — her ally now possessed by the same darkness that swallowed the Mother — and manages to purify her. In the fullest ending, the two escape together with unexpected help from the Knight, Hornet's silent sibling from the original Hollow Knight, closing out Silksong's story with Pharloom's ancient curse broken but its future left uncertain.
